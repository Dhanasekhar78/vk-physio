Meet Dr. Dharmateja Challagiri (PT)

Dr. Dharmateja Challagiri leads VK Physiotherapy Clinic on the National Highway near Venkatachalam Toll Plaza. He holds a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) and a Master of Physiotherapy in Musculoskeletal Science (MPT), and is a member of the Indian Association of Physiotherapists (MIAP).

His focus is on the muscles, joints and spine: assessing the cause of your pain, treating it with proven physiotherapy methods and guiding you with exercises so that relief lasts.
